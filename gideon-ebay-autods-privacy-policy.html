Privacy Policy

Gideon eBay AutoDS Bot

Effective date: September 3, 2026

Overview

Gideon eBay AutoDS Bot is a private store-management application used to connect with eBay APIs and related ecommerce services for authorized store operations, analytics, inventory review, fulfillment monitoring, and product-management workflows.

Information the application may access

When authorized by the user, the application may access eBay account information permitted by the OAuth scopes granted by the user. This can include inventory and listing data, account settings, fulfillment and order information, selling analytics, marketplace insights, and catalog information.

How information is used

Information accessed through eBay APIs is used only to operate, monitor, analyze, and improve the user's own ecommerce store workflows. The application does not sell personal information or use eBay account data for advertising.

Authentication and credentials

API credentials, OAuth access tokens, and refresh tokens are intended to be stored securely and used only for authenticated communication with eBay and other connected services. Passwords are not collected by this application.

Data sharing

Data is not sold to third parties. Information may be transmitted to authorized service providers only as necessary to operate the ecommerce workflow, such as eBay, AutoDS, and supported suppliers or fulfillment services selected by the user.

Data retention

The application may retain operational records such as product evaluations, listing status, order-monitoring results, and audit logs for store-management purposes. Sensitive credentials should be retained only as long as necessary for the authorized connection.

User control and revocation

The user can revoke the application's access through eBay account permissions or by revoking the relevant OAuth authorization. After revocation, the application can no longer access new eBay account data using that authorization.

Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect stored credentials and application data. No method of electronic storage or transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated as the application adds or changes features. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions related to Gideon eBay AutoDS Bot, contact the application owner at the email address associated with the application's eBay Developers Program registration.
